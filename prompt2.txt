MODEL 2 SPEC (FIXED; put it in configs/models.yaml under bigru_attn, read everything from it)
- Input: text_clean. Tokenizer: regex \w+|[^\w\s] applied to the LOWERCASED text. Vocab: top 50k TRAIN tokens (min_freq=2, built from the full train split even in --smoke) plus <pad>=0 and <unk>=1; log token coverage (token-weighted) and warn, do not abort, if it differs from ~98.6%. max_len=256, keep the FIRST 256 tokens (truncate the tail). Log the % of val and test comments truncated.
- Embedding: fastText 300-d, FROZEN. <pad> = zeros. Words missing from fastText (and <unk>) get random vectors drawn from a normal with the per-dimension mean and std of the found vectors. Log the fastText hit rate (vocab types and token-weighted). Spatial dropout 0.25 (drop whole embedding channels across time).
- Encoder: 1-layer bidirectional GRU, 128 hidden per direction (256 out), dropout 0.3 on the GRU output. Use pack_padded_sequence (enforce_sorted=False) so padding never enters either direction.
- Attention: additive attention pooling (tanh projection, attention dim 128, learned context vector), masked over padded positions, softmax computed in float32. forward(..., return_attn=True) must be supported.
- Head: Linear 256 -> 6 producing logits; sigmoid at inference. Log trainable vs frozen parameter counts (expect about 0.4M trainable).
- Imbalance: weighted BCEWithLogits with pos_weight_l = (neg/pos)^0.5 per label from TRAIN (same recipe as Model 1, log the values), plus head bias initialised to log(pos_rate/(1-pos_rate)) per label.
- Training: AdamW (lr 1e-3, weight_decay 0.01 on trainable params only), batch size 128, grad clipping 1.0, up to 6 epochs, no LR scheduler. Early stopping on val macro PR-AUC (average precision), patience 2; restore the best checkpoint before predicting. Seed everything (cuDNN GRU is not bit-deterministic; note it in limitations).
