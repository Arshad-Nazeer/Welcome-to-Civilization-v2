MODEL 3: DistilRoBERTa-base
Tokens: RoBERTa byte-level BPE (vocab 50,265), case preserved. max_len 128, head truncation, dynamic padding. Eval batches are length-sorted. Train batches are length-bucketed with shuffled bucket order, so batches differ each epoch.
Embedding: learned token embeddings (768-d) plus learned position embeddings, fine-tuned.
Encoder: 6 transformer layers, 12 heads, hidden 768, FFN 3072, about 82M parameters.
Head: HF classification head on the <s> token (768->768, tanh, dropout 0.1, then 768->6). The built-in loss is ignored (labels are never passed to the model).

Training:
- AdamW, weight decay 0.01 (not applied to bias and LayerNorm), 10% warmup then linear decay.
- Batch 32, fp16 autocast with gradient scaling, gradient clip 1.0, loss computed in float32, deterministic, seed 42.
- Layer-wise LR decay 0.9 (head LR 3e-5, lower layers decayed); decay factor is tuned.
- 4 epochs. Evaluate on val every epoch; keep the best epoch by val macro PR-AUC (not loss). Resumable checkpoints.

Imbalance (extreme): threat is 0.30% positive (~380 train examples); severe_toxic and identity_hate ~1%. Four layers, none touching test:
1. Damped weighted BCE: pos_weight = min((neg/pos)^p, cap), start p=0.45, cap 14.
2. Log-odds bias init on the final 768->6 layer.
3. Optional focal term (1-p_t)^gamma, gamma=0 default (off), 1-2 tuned. If gamma>0, lower pos_weight power (~0.3) to avoid double-counting.
4. Optional mild oversampling of comments carrying threat/severe_toxic/identity_hate: sampler weight 1+(k-1)*[has rare label], k in {1,2,4}, k=1 off. If k>1, reduce pos_weight so the same positives are not counted twice.
